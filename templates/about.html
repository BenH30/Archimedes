Who We Are

Archimedes Metals is a family-owned decorative metal fabrication company serving commercial construction, architecture, design, and specialty trade partners across Dallas–Fort Worth and beyond.

Our roots are in custom, performance-driven fabrication, where precision, durability, and accountability are not optional. That background shaped how we approach architectural and decorative metalwork today. We are not interested in becoming a commodity metal shop. We are building a company known for design sensitivity, clear communication, exceptional craftsmanship, and the ability to solve problems that don't fit neatly into a catalog.

Whether we are fabricating signage, panels, screens, enclosures, shelving, trim, brass features, formed components, welded assemblies, or specialty details, our goal is the same: understand the intent, communicate clearly, build it right, and deliver a result the project team is proud to put their name on.

"Fabricating a World Worth Looking At."

This tagline captures the way Archimedes wants to show up in the market: as a team that cares deeply about design, quality, craftsmanship, and the experience created by the finished work.
